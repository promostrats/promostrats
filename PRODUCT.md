## Platform

web

## Users

Owners/decision-makers at UK independent brands and small businesses — especially Shopify store owners — who are evaluating whether to hire someone to build a new website or fix problems (structure, usability, SEO) on the one they already have. They are comparing a solo freelancer against agencies and DIY/theme-store options.

## Product Purpose

PromoStrats is Connor Bawcutt's freelance web design, development and SEO practice, based in London (East Dulwich). It builds custom websites from scratch and fixes structural, usability and SEO issues on existing sites — with Shopify as a specialism, not an exclusivity. Success is a site that "works properly": found by search engines, usable by customers, and converting visitors into sales.

## Positioning

Solo practitioner, not an agency: "the person on the call is the person doing the work." Confirmed by user. Differentiators: direct access to the person doing the work throughout; clear pricing quoted before any work starts; fixes done properly at the source (custom-built theme sections, structured SEO) rather than plugins stacked on plugins.

## Operating Context

Client journey: free strategy call (site reviewed live, no obligation) → written recommendations with cost → implementation inside the client's existing setup with regular updates → before/after summary plus next-step suggestions. Site also offers a free, no-signup automated site checker (SEO + design/UX scoring with findings) as a lead-in tool, and a portfolio of real case studies with before/after image comparisons.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Contact form submits via Formspree; no backend beyond that.
- Free site-check tool runs an automated audit (SEO + design/UX scores, findings list) — must remain functional.
- Portfolio uses a drag-to-compare before/after image slider per case study; real project screenshots exist in `public/images/`.
- Homepage currently features an animated "site-audit.log" terminal-style demo as its hero visual centerpiece. User confirmed they are open to replacing this concept entirely in the redesign — it is not a fixed brand asset, just the current execution.
- Legal pages (privacy, terms) and analytics (GA4) must be preserved as-is functionally.

## Product Principles

1. Prove competence through real, specific work (before/after case studies, live audit tooling) rather than generic claims.
2. Keep the solo-freelancer trust signals prominent: direct contact, transparent pricing, no-pressure free call.
3. Every page should make the next step (book a call) obvious without being pushy.
4. Technical credibility matters to this audience — the site itself should read as competently built (fast, accessible, no stock-template feel).
